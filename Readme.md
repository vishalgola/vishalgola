## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm **Vishal Prajapati**, an AI/ML practitioner focused on building intelligent applications that combine machine learning, Generative AI, and software engineering.

My interests span the entire AI development lifecycle — from data preprocessing and model development to LLM-powered applications, agentic workflows, API development, and ML pipeline automation.

I enjoy turning ideas into working systems, experimenting with different approaches, and understanding how to make AI applications more reliable, maintainable, and useful.

- 🤖 **AI & Machine Learning:** Predictive modeling, feature engineering, model evaluation, and deep learning.
- 🧠 **Generative AI:** LLM applications, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), embeddings, and vector search.
- 🔗 **AI Agents:** LangChain, LangGraph, tool integration, workflow orchestration, and human-in-the-loop systems.
- ⚙️ **MLOps & Deployment:** MLflow, Docker, FastAPI, CI/CD, and model-serving workflows.
- 👁️ **Computer Vision:** OpenCV, image processing, and deep learning applications.
- 📊 **Data Science:** Exploratory data analysis, statistical analysis, and data-driven decision-making.
- 🎓 **Education:** B.Tech in Robotics and Artificial Intelligence at J.C. Bose University of Science and Technology, YMCA.

> My focus is not just training models — it's building complete AI solutions that can be tested, deployed, and improved.
